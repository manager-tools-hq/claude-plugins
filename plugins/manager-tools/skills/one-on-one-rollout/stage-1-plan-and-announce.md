# Stage 1: Plan and announce

1. **Directs.** Ask for each direct's name, email, whether they work in the manager's location or remotely, and whether they're joining a team that already has One on Ones. Don't ask how the manager signs emails: read a few of their recent sent emails, see how they sign off, and confirm it in one line ("You sign off as 'Michael' — I'll use that, OK?").
   - Situation: `new_direct_joining` if the team already has One on Ones and only new people need them; `new_team` or `taking_over_team` if the manager says so; otherwise `first_rollout`.
2. **Calendar.** Take the time zone from the manager's calendar. List every event from today through seven weeks out; these are `manager_busy` (pass times as the calendar returns them; an all-day event ends the next day).
   - From the recurring events, suggest which is the boss's staff meeting, the manager's One on One with their boss, and the manager's own staff meeting. Ask the manager to confirm or correct each; any can be "none".
   - Ask whether they regularly leave early on any day.
   - Assume Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and say so; use what the manager tells you instead.
3. **Where the slots go.** Call `plan_one_on_one_rollout` without `slot_preference`. Tell the manager how many open weekly slots each day has (`free_slots_by_day`) and ask: focus on particular days (which?) or spread over the week? Call again with their answer as `slot_preference`.
4. **The plan.** Show the start week, the offered slots, the dates that matter (holds and announcement, replies due, schedule accepted and invitations sent, first One on Ones), any warnings and assumptions, and the evidence as titles with links. If a warning says too few slots were free, help the manager free time or widen hours, then plan again. Get approval before anything else.
5. **Holds.** List the holds and get a yes. Create each as a weekly 30-minute event, starting on that weekday in the first week, with no guests, titled and described per the markers (rollout stamp = now), in the manager's time zone.
6. **Announcement.** Call `draft_rollout_message` with `announcement_email`, `start_date` = the first week, the offered slots, and the manager's name. Show the subject and body exactly. On a yes, send it from the manager's Gmail to all the directs. Then label the sent thread `One on One rollout: active`, creating the label if needed. If labelling fails, tell the manager; the rollout can still be found from the announcement and the holds (see the markers in `SKILL.md`).
7. **Briefing.** Call `draft_rollout_message` with `briefing_outline`, `start_date`, and `has_staff_meeting` (true if they confirmed their own staff meeting). Give the manager the outline.
8. **Next.** Say when replies are due and that "check on my One on One rollout" picks it up from here.

**A new direct joining a team that already has One on Ones:** plan with `new_direct_joining`, hold the slots, and skip the team announcement, the Manager Tools template, and the briefing. The manager briefs the new direct in person. Write a short email to the new direct only, subject `Your One-On-One time`, offering the held slots and asking for a reply with their pick. Show it, send it on a yes, and label that thread `One on One rollout: active`. Their reply is due by the first week, not ten days before it. Stage 2 and Stage 3 then work as usual, without the team schedule email.
