# Reset

1. **Show.** Find the current rollout (see the markers in `SKILL.md`; it may be holds with no announcement yet). List its announcement date, who replied, and the holds and meetings that carry its rollout stamp. Search the calendar from the rollout date to seven weeks after it.
2. **Ask.** Holds are always removed. Ask whether to remove the meetings too: yes for a test or a clean restart; a manager restarting for real may keep them until a new schedule replaces them.
3. **Remove and close.** On a yes, delete those events as whole series, then replace the thread's `One on One rollout: active` label with `One on One rollout: closed`. If labels can't be changed, send the manager (only) an email titled `One on One rollout reset`, saying the rollout announced on that date was reset.
4. **Nothing to the directs.** Say that a fresh start begins with Stage 1 and its new announcement.
